Project Monkeyface Defect Risk Explorer
Quality Assurance · Phase A

Which weather conditions line up with the strawberry defect?

Strawberries sometimes grow deformed — a defect called monkey-face (or cat-face). This tool checks the real weather each field experienced while its fruit was forming, then shows which conditions are associated with more defects — so you can tell whether a bad season could have been seen coming, and which suppliers to watch.

How it works: first format your field exports into one clean layout, then analyze them — the tool pulls each field's actual weather automatically and finds the patterns. No weather data needed on your end.

Defect rate vs. the top condition
