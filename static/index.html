Which weather conditions cause the strawberry defect?

Strawberries sometimes grow deformed — a defect called monkey-face (or cat-face). This tool checks the real weather each field experienced while its fruit was forming, then shows which conditions line up with more defects — so you can tell whether a bad season could have been seen coming, and which suppliers to watch.

How it works: you give it a spreadsheet of fields (where and when each was harvested); it pulls that field's actual weather automatically and finds the patterns. No weather data needed on your end.
